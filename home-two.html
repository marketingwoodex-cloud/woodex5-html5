Design and build

One contract for the whole floor

Fit-out is where projects lose money — through unclear scope boundaries, landlord conditions nobody read, and services that clash with the ceiling design.

We take a single contract, coordinate the landlord's base build, and publish a weekly programme everyone can hold us to.

  • Shell-and-core condition surveyLandlord liaison, permits and reinstatement handled by us
  • MEP design and coordinationHVAC, power, data and fire coordinated before the ceiling closes
  • In-house joinery manufacture22,000 sq ft workshop, ±1.5 mm tolerance, factory dry-assembly
  • Testing, commissioning and handoverSnag list agreed together and closed within fourteen days

The way we work

From survey to snag list

Six stages, each with defined outputs you sign off before we move on.

The full process
01
Listen · Week 1

Brief & site survey

A 60-minute consultation, a measured survey and an honest read on the building — including the things that will cost you money if ignored.

  • Measured drawings
  • Existing-conditions report
  • Budget range
02
Plan · Weeks 2 – 4

Concept & space planning

Layout options tested against how you actually use the space: circulation, daylight, storage, headcount and the view from the door.

  • Two layout options
  • Zoning diagram
  • Furniture plan
03
Design · Weeks 4 – 7

Design development

Materials, lighting and joinery resolved to specification. Every decision written down with a code, a supplier and a price attached.

  • Finishes schedule
  • Lighting layout & scenes
  • Joinery design intent
04
See · Weeks 7 – 9

3D visualisation & approval

Photoreal views built from the same model the workshop uses, so the image you approve is the geometry we cut.

  • Photoreal stills
  • Optional walkthrough
  • Two revision rounds
05
Draw · Weeks 9 – 11

Construction & shop drawings

Coordinated MEP, reflected ceilings, partitions and full joinery shop drawings issued for manufacture — the set your landlord approves too.

  • Coordinated MEP drawings
  • Reflected ceiling plan
  • Joinery shop drawings
06
Build · Weeks 11 – 30

Manufacture, site & handover

Workshop production runs in parallel with site works. Friday photo reports, a resident supervisor, and a snag list closed before the final payment.

  • Weekly photo report
  • Factory inspection notes
  • Testing & commissioning
