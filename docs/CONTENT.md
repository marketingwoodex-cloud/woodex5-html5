# Content Guide

## Tone

Premium but human. Clear, calm, specific, and useful.

Avoid unsupported claims such as “best,” “luxury,” “award-winning,” or exact project metrics unless approved.

## Primary positioning

Design, build, furniture, and handover under one considered process.

## Core proof

- 3D approval before construction
- Clear scope before ordering
- One accountable team
- Design and build connected
- Detailed handover

## Required business approvals

- Market: Pakistan or Pakistan + UAE
- Official contact information
- WhatsApp number
- Service areas
- Project facts
- Testimonials
- Client logos
- Warranty terms
- Pricing language
