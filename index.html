Why in-house manufacture matters

The drawing is a request. The jig is a guarantee.

Most fit-out problems are translation problems: a designer draws something, a third-party carpenter interprets it, and the interpretation is what gets installed. Owning the workshop removes the translation.

Shop drawings come from the same model as the 3D you approved. Every panel is numbered, dry-assembled in the factory, photographed and signed off before it travels to site.

  • 01Shop drawings issued from the approved 3D model — one source of truth
  • 02Factory dry-assembly and photographic sign-off before dispatch
  • 03Our own installers, not a day-rate crew hired for the week
  • 04Ten-year structural warranty on everything we manufacture
