What a fair project timeline really looks like

Weeks, not vibes: the honest phasing of a residential or fit-out project — and where delays actually come from.

Hina Tariq

Senior Project Manager

May 4, 2026 · 5 min read

Every client asks how long. Few are shown a real answer. This is the schedule we publish at kickoff — the same one our project managers are measured against.

  • Weeks 1–2: discovery, measure, scope agreement
  • Weeks 3–5: concept and layout decisions
  • Weeks 6–9: detailed design, 3D approval, fixed quotation
  • Weeks 6–12 (parallel): joinery fabrication in our workshop
  • Weeks 10–20: site works, services, finishes
  • Final week: install, snag, deep clean, handover

Where delays really come from

In six years of project management, my delay ledger reads: late client decisions (most), hidden site conditions (second), imported material shipping (third). Only one of those three is invisible at kickoff — so we front-load decision deadlines and order imports the day drawings are approved.

A schedule is a promise with dates on it. If your studio cannot show you one in week one, ask why.
