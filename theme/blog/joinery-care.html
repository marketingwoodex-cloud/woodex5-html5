Caring for solid wood joinery — a ten-minute annual ritual

Re-oiling, honest cleaning and the three things that void warranties — from the person who signs ours.

Bilal Khan

Joinery Workshop Lead

Apr 15, 2026 · 3 min read

Solid wood joinery is the only surface in your home that improves with age — if you give it ten minutes a year. Here is the ritual I give every client at handover.

Every week

Dry or barely-damp cotton cloth, following the grain. No sprays, no silicone polishes — they seal the wood and stop future oiling from taking.

Once a year

Re-oil oiled surfaces with the care kit we hand over: thin coat, twenty minutes, wipe everything off with the grain. Kitchens and vanities may want twice a year near water and hobs.

The three warranty-voiders

  • Standing water left on any joint for hours
  • Steam mops or wet cloths on veneer and laminate edges
  • Harsh chemical cleaners on oiled or waxed finishes

Do these three things and your joinery will outlive the fashion it was built in. Every Woodex piece carries a ten-year warranty — and most will be re-oiled by grandchildren.
