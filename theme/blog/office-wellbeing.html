Why your office should feel like a good café

Acoustics, daylight and choice — the three things knowledge workers actually rank above table football.

Usman Sheikh

3D & Visualisation Lead

Jun 2, 2026 · 5 min read

After our Clearline office project, we surveyed the teams six months in. The features they valued most were not the games room — they were the three below, in order.

1. The ability to choose your setting

Focus booths, standing benches, soft lounges, a proper café table. People do their best work when the setting matches the task — so give them four settings within a twenty-second walk.

2. Acoustic comfort

Noise is the number one complaint in open offices. Slatted timber, felt panels, soft seating and zoning cut reverberation to conversational levels — measure it, do not guess it.

3. Daylight you can work beside

Desks perpendicular to windows, glare controlled with fabric rather than blinds, and darker break-out corners so the eye can rest. Café-grade daylight is a retention strategy.

Staff who choose their setting report 30% higher focus scores. The games room came fifth — behind plants.
