Seven tricks we use in small apartments

Full-height joinery, borrowed light and one brave colour — how we make 900 sq.ft live like 1,400.

Sana Malik

Design Director

Jun 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Small apartments reward decisiveness. These are the moves we repeat project after project in 800–1,000 sq.ft apartments across Lahore and Islamabad.

  • Take joinery full height — it draws the eye up and doubles real storage
  • One material for the largest surfaces; variety only in small doses
  • Doors that pocket or slide free up a square metre each
  • A single 'hero' wall — colour, texture or joinery — instead of many small gestures
  • Mirrors opposite windows to borrow light, never opposite clutter
  • Furniture with legs and visible floor underneath keeps rooms breathing
  • Curves in furniture navigate tight circulation more gracefully than corners

The rule of one hero

Every small space gets one hero moment — a joinery wall, a colour, a stone — and everything else stays quiet. Two heroes halve each other.

In our Ridge Apartment project, the hero was a full-height oak storage wall that hid the TV, desk, wardrobe and pantry behind five flush doors. Everything else in the apartment is linen and white — and the apartment photographs like a showroom.
