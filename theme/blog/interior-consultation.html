What actually happens in an interior consultation?

No mystique, no obligation — a walkthrough of our first ninety minutes together and what you leave with.

Maryam Nawaz

Client Relations

Jul 21, 2026 · 5 min read

People often arrive at a first consultation expecting to be sold something. Ours is closer to a diagnosis. Here is exactly what happens.

The first thirty minutes: listening

We walk your space and ask about routines, not styles: who cooks, who works from home, where the clutter collects, what the current space makes hard. Styles emerge from habits — never the other way round.

The next thirty: numbers

We talk budget openly, in ranges, with real per-square-foot figures from comparable projects. If your brief and budget do not meet, we say so in the first meeting and show what a phased approach could look like instead.

The last thirty: next steps

You leave with a written summary: scope options, honest price ranges, a rough timeline — and space to think. No deposit, no pressure, no countdown discount. Good projects start with unforced decisions.

The best consultations end with the client saying 'we finally know what to do' — whether or not they hire us.
