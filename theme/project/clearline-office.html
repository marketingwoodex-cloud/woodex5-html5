About this project

A growing tech firm needed a headquarters that matched how engineering teams actually work: deep focus blocks, noisy collaboration and quiet calls — all at once. The floor plate is zoned into acoustic bands, with workshop-built oak desk clusters and phone booths between.

Amenities — café, library, games room — were placed at the building's heart so no one is more than fifteen steps from a reset. Occupancy satisfaction measured 4.7/5 three months in.
