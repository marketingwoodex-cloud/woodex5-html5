Dusk Lounge

A dinner lounge tuned to 8pm — low light, deep banquettes, perfect acoustics.

Completed

About this project

Nomad wanted the room guests choose for anniversaries. We wrapped the lounge in oak slats and bouclé banquettes, dropped the lighting to candle level with dimmable scenes, and tuned the ceiling so a whisper carries but a full room never roars.

The bar — fluted oak under a brass canopy — anchors the room and turns three covers a night. Rebuilt in eleven weeks while the kitchen kept trading.
