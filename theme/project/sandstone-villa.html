About this project

Two years abroad, one summer to move in. The family approved every room — from the pool terrace's pergola shadow to the pantry drawer runners — inside our 3D model, then landed to a finished home.

The architecture's stone and plaster language continues indoors: sandstone floors, oak joinery, linen curtains and a sunken lounge arranged around the valley view. Fully documented under our ten-year joinery warranty.
