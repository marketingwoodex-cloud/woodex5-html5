About this project

Clients in Dubai, a bedroom in Lahore, zero flights. Measurements came from our local site team; decisions happened on video walkthroughs; approvals happened inside the 3D model. The palette — linen, pale oak, brushed brass — was sampled by courier.

The suite ships as a complete remote package: drawings, joinery, furniture list and install supervision. The reveal matched the approved renders to the millimetre.
