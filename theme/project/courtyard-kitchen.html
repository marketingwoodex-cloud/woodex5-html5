About this project

The brief was one line: cook facing the garden. We rebuilt the room around a fluted oak run with a travertine island, hiding the pantry, appliances and a breakfast bar behind full-height joinery that reads as furniture.

Every drawer is specified to what it holds — spices, pots, school lunchboxes — and the brass hardware was chosen to age with use. Fabricated and installed in six weeks.
