About this project

A 1-kanal villa with generous bones and a layout that fought the family at every turn. We opened the ground floor into one continuous day-lit space — kitchen, dining and lounge arranged around a new oak stair — and gave every bedroom its own dressing joinery.

The material palette was kept deliberately quiet: white-oiled oak, lime-washed walls, travertine and brass, so the family's collection of art and books does the talking. Delivered turnkey in nineteen weeks.
