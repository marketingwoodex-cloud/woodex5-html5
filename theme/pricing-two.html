Why the range?

Three things move the number

01

Joinery scope

The single biggest lever. A full kitchen run of bespoke oak moves the number more than any finish.

02

Material grade

Imported stone and solid wood versus certified plywood and local stone — both beautiful, different budgets.

03

Site condition

Builder-finish versus bare shell changes the works package. We price it after measuring, never before.
