Before you brief us

The twelve questions we are asked most often about scope, price and programme.

Still unresolved?

It begins with a conversation, not a quotation. Send your floor plan, a rough budget and the date you need the space by. We reply within one business day with an honest view of whether we are the right studio for it, and if we are, we book a 60-minute consultation — free, in person or on video.

Getting started

No. The first consultation is free and there is no obligation afterwards. We use it to understand the brief, walk the site if it is in Lahore, and give you a budget range. Concept design after that point is a paid stage, because it involves measured surveys and real design time.

Getting started

Three things help most: an existing floor plan or measured sketch, four to eight images of spaces you like (including one you do not like, which is often more useful), and a target completion date. If you have a society or landlord handbook, send that too — it usually changes the programme.

Getting started

Yes. We deliver regularly in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad and Multan, and take on Karachi projects above a certain scale. Outside Lahore we add a mobilisation line to the proposal covering travel, supervision stays and logistics, and we appoint a resident site supervisor rather than commuting the team daily.

Getting started

As a line-item bill of quantities, not a square-foot lump sum. You see each trade, each material and each fixture separately, with quantities taken from the drawings. That is why our budget variance stays under three percent — there is nowhere for an allowance to hide.

Pricing

For fit-out work in Pakistan, residential typically lands between PKR 1,400 and 2,400 per square foot, commercial between PKR 2,200 and 3,400, and hospitality between PKR 3,000 and 4,200 depending on services and finishes. Design fees are quoted separately and are usually eight to fourteen percent of build cost.

Pricing

Commonly 25% on mobilisation, monthly progress payments measured against the programme, and 5% retention released at the end of the defect-liability period. For smaller joinery-only commissions we work to 50% on order and 50% on installation. Everything is written into the contract before work starts.

Pricing

Every change is priced and signed before it is built. You receive a one-page variation note showing the cost, the programme impact and the drawing it affects. We do not carry verbal instructions onto site, and we do not present surprise invoices at handover.

Pricing

Yes, and it is the healthiest way to start. Give us the number you cannot exceed and we design to it — value-engineering materials and scope in that order, never by removing the things that make the space work.

Pricing

Design runs three to six weeks for a home or a small commercial unit, and six to ten weeks for a full floor or restaurant. Construction then runs from four weeks for a single-room renovation to twenty-four weeks for a large turnkey home. We publish the whole programme at contract stage and track it weekly.

Process & timeline

Five: measured survey and existing-conditions report, concept and space planning, design development with material and lighting schedules, construction drawings with joinery shop drawings, and 3D visualisation for your approval. You sign off each stage before we move to the next.
