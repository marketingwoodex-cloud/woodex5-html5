2. Fit-Out
3. Hospitality
4. Specialist
5. Studio

## Mega-menu: all 15 services

The navigation can expose all services, but each item should link to a dedicated detail page instead of sending every item to one generic Services page.

# Content quality audit

## Strong differentiators found

- Design, build, and deliver under one process
- 3D approval before construction
- Fixed scope and itemized pricing language
- One accountable team
- Custom furniture and joinery
- 3D visualization studio
- Commercial, residential, retail, and hospitality capability
- Process-led handover

## Claims requiring verification

- 500+ projects
- 15 years of experience
- 98% on-time delivery
- 5-year workmanship warranty
- Render-accuracy guarantee
- Own workshop
- Own build team
- 6–9 week office fit-out timeline
- 24–48 hour reply promise
- Any AED pricing
- UAE phone number
- Client logos

## Pakistan market recommendation

For the Pakistan version, replace UAE-specific details with:

- Confirmed Pakistani cities served
- PKR or quotation-on-request language
- Official Pakistan WhatsApp number
- Local project examples
- Pakistan-specific material and construction context
- Local response time
- Approved local client proof

---

# Recommended service detail page structure

Every service page should contain:

1. Service hero
2. Human problem statement
3. Woodex response
4. What is included
5. What affects cost
6. What the client receives
7. Process steps
8. Timeline factors
9. Related projects
10. FAQs
11. WhatsApp CTA
12. Consultation form link

**Send us your floor plan.**

## Recommended secondary CTA

**Chat with Woodex on WhatsApp.**

# Final audit conclusion

Woodex currently offers a strong full-service interior design and delivery portfolio. The offer is broad enough for residential, commercial, retail, hospitality, fit-out, furniture, and visualization work.

The main service-page improvement is not adding more services. It is organizing the current offer clearly, adding dedicated detail pages, and replacing unverified UAE-specific claims with approved Pakistan-specific information.
