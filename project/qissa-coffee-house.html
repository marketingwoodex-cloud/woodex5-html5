Qissa Coffee House

A 1,450 sq ft café built around a three-metre counter, a queue line that never blocks the door, and acoustics that survive a full house.

The brief and how we answered it

Qissa wanted a neighbourhood café with the throughput of a city store. The design starts at the espresso machine and works outwards to seating, queue and street.

The challenge

A 1,450 sq ft shell with a single service riser, a 60-cover ambition, and a neighbouring residential floor that objects to noise after 11pm.

Our approach

We modelled the queue at 42 covers per hour, moved the pastry case to break the line, specified absorbent ceiling rafts and a 2400K evening scene, and routed extraction through a silencer box on the rear elevation.

The result

Seven weeks from site start to first pour. Average dwell time settled at 47 minutes with turnover maintained at 38 covers per hour through the morning peak.
